# 2026-10-11: v1.0.0 (scan correction, annotation defaults)

Follows `2026-10-10-v0.5.12.md`. Released as a major version at the user's request.

## Scan correction

- **Straighten (deskew):** File ▸ Straighten Scanned Pages… (`StraightenDialog.svelte`),
  the palette, or right-click ▸ Straighten Page. `session.straightenPages` measures each
  page's tilt with `measureSkew` and corrects pages tilted 0.1° or more. The dialog's
  re-OCR box starts ticked when the chosen pages had text. The right-click version has
  no dialog and re-OCRs automatically if the page had text.
- **Perspective:** File ▸ Correct Perspective…, the palette, or right-click.
  `session.startPerspective` sets `session.perspective`, and `PageView` then shows
  `PerspectiveOverlay.svelte`: four draggable corners (arrow keys nudge them) pre-placed
  by `detectCorners`, with Apply/Enter or Cancel/Escape. `session.correctPerspective`
  does the work.
- **Shared pipeline:** `#applyCorrections`. `scan/correct.ts` renders the page as seen
  at 300 DPI (`renderPageImage`, now shared with OCR in `pdf/render.ts`) and either
  rotates it on a canvas or warps it through a homography with bilinear sampling, then
  writes a 0.9-quality JPEG. Each `CorrectedPage` comes with a `mapPoint` that carries
  old PDF coordinates into the new page; annotations on corrected pages are moved with
  `transformAnnot` (`annotations/types.ts`). After `buildSavedPdf`,
  `annotations/replacePage.ts` swaps each page's content for the image (new MediaBox,
  stale boxes dropped, Rotate 0, fresh Resources) and the result is reopened through
  `#reloadTransformed`.
- **Measuring:** `scan/analysis.ts` (no DOM, tested in Node).
  - `detectSkew` uses projection profiles: a 0.25° sweep across ±10°, then a 0.05°
    refinement. Positive means lines rise to the right.
  - `detectDocumentQuad` takes the largest bright region after an Otsu threshold and its
    extreme points along the diagonals. It returns null when the region is under 15% of
    the frame or its corners sit on the frame's corners (a flatbed scan).
  - Ink is `<= threshold`: Otsu's threshold belongs to the dark class.
- `scan/geometry.ts`: `homography`, `project`, `rotateAbout`, and `sheetSizeFor`, which
  snaps to A4, Letter or Legal within 4% and otherwise sets the long side to 11 inches.
- **Limits:** corner detection needs paper lighter than the background. Annotations and
  form fields that pdf.js draws straight from the file (not in our model) are not moved.

## Annotation defaults

- **Use as default:** properties panel ▸ "Use as default" → `viewer.useAsDefault(annot)`.
  `styleOf` (`state/viewer.svelte.ts`) is the inverse of the `create*` factories, and
  only returns the fields a kind uses.
- **Settings ▸ Annotation defaults:** `StyleDefaults.svelte` edits `viewer.styles`
  directly, the same store the toolbar uses, with Reset per kind and Reset all
  (`resetStyle`, `resetAllStyles`).

## Tests

- New fixture `scan.pdf` (`scripts/fixtures/make-test-pdf.ts`): page 1 has text tilted
  3°; page 2 is an A4-shaped light sheet at an angle on a dark background.
- `verify:roundtrip`: skew detection at 3°, −2°, 0° and on a blank page; corner
  detection on a synthetic photo and on a full-frame page; the homography; A4 snapping;
  the rotation direction; `transformAnnot`; and `replaceWithImage` checked through
  pdf.js.
- `verify:ui`: 137/137, including Use as default → text tool, Settings Reset all,
  right-click Straighten (3.0°, then re-OCR reads the words back), and perspective
  corners detected within 2% and turned into an A4 page.
